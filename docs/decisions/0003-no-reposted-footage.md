# 3. No reposted footage

Date: 2026-07-24
Status: accepted

## Context

The original concept was to fetch popular funny videos, add text, and republish.
Research (docs/research-2026.md sections 2 and 4) found all three platforms
penalise this: YouTube's July 2025 inauthentic-content policy, TikTok's
originality policy, Instagram's aggregator downranking. Plus Content ID and DMCA
exposure. Posting to Reddit does not transfer copyright.

## Decision

All footage is CC0 stock (Pexels/Pixabay), public domain, or self-recorded.
No yt-dlp against YouTube/TikTok. Reddit is a text source only, and story text
must be rewritten rather than narrated verbatim.

## Consequences

Rules out the fastest-to-build concepts. In exchange the accounts stay eligible
for distribution and monetisation, which is the whole point of the exercise.
